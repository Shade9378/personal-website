  - title: "CHEM 120 Organic Chemistry E-Textbook"
    abstract: >-
      Organic chemistry is a very visual subject, as organic compounds, which are the focus of CHEM120, are three-dimensional objects. Understanding their composition, their properties, and their chemistry will require the students to understand their 3D shapes. However, we often need to represent organic compounds on 2D surfaces - a piece of paper, a computer screen, or a whiteboard. One of the biggest challenges the students encounter throughout this process will be to learn how to represent these molecules in 2D while having a 3D understanding of them. Our etextbook is an attempt to address this challenge by taking full advantage of the digital medium by incorporating into it not only images, but video, games, 3D interactive objects and more.
